# Novomins - Brand Strategy Report

*Prepared 2026-10-06. Markets: UK (home market), EU (Ireland, Germany/Austria), US. Australia is noted because it is a big part of the brand's international story. No Spate export was supplied and Revuze was not used. The brand website (novomins.com) and most press pages could not be opened directly from this environment. Facts were taken from search-engine summaries of those pages, retailer listings and regulatory registers, and each is cited to the underlying URL. Treat exact wording as "as reported in search snippets" until the pages are checked.*

**Brand identification.** "Novomins" here means Novomins Nutrition, the gummy vitamin brand founded in 2020 by Dr Peter McQuillan and Akil Memishi. It is reported to trade as Innocent Health Limited, with an office at 405 King's Road, London SW10 ([B Corp directory](https://www.bcorporation.net//find-a-b-corp/company/innocent-health-limited); [PlantX/retailer snippet](https://plantx.uk/products/novomins-calcium-gummies-gummies)). Do not confuse it with Novoma (a separate UK supplement brand on Trustpilot), Novonesis (an ingredients company) or Novo Nordisk. Evidence for the match: the same founders, the gummy-only range and the same retailer listings appear across all sources below.

**Evidence tags:** CONFIRMED = stated by the brand or solid data; REPORTED = credible third party; INFERRED = analyst reasoning; USER-SUPPLIED = from the user.

## Summary

Novomins is a bootstrapped UK gummy vitamin brand founded in 2020 with £70k of the founders' savings. It sells a clinician-founded "make the healthy choice the obvious choice" promise through a very wide, mostly women-focused gummy range. Its sales sequence was DTC and Amazon UK first, then from late 2022 a fast move into bricks-and-mortar: Chemist Warehouse Ireland (Nov 2022), Boots, Superdrug and Holland & Barrett in the UK (about 20,000 distribution points claimed), and 550 Chemist Warehouse stores in Australia (c. 2025). Its success is visible but mostly self-reported. Revenue was £2.5m and then £3.5m within about two years (2022-23, company figures in the press), it ranked #27 in the Sunday Times 100 in June 2026, and it claims "a tub every 10 seconds". No audited current revenue was found. The most plausible drivers are four: being early to a gummy-only, need-state-led range for women; doctor-founder credibility; a fast move from DTC proof to pharmacy and health-store retail; and taste and format solving pill fatigue. The weak points are range sprawl into trend SKUs (NAD+, shilajit, libido, "Endo"), claims risk on disease-adjacent naming, and a thin, possibly unofficial US presence.

## 1. Brand story

- **Origin.** Founded in 2020 by Dr Peter McQuillan (dental surgeon, University of Glasgow BDS; London's Best Young Dentist 2018) and Akil Memishi (BSc Hons Pharmacology, Glasgow; described as a cancer researcher, pharmacologist and neuroscientist; Co-founder and Chief Scientific Officer). REPORTED ([Insider/Business Insider Scotland](https://www.insider.co.uk/news/glasgow-graduates-create-growing-vitamin-28794580); [University of Glasgow alumni](https://www.gla.ac.uk/alumni/noticeboard/headline_917794_en.html); [TheOrg](https://theorg.com/org/novomins-nutrition/org-chart/akil-memishi))
- **Founding myth.** When the pair were moving house in their final year at university, Memishi noticed that "90% of the vitamin tubs" in McQuillan's kitchen were out of date or unfinished. A few years later they settled on gummies. REPORTED ([Insider](https://www.insider.co.uk/news/glasgow-graduates-create-growing-vitamin-28794580))
- **Funding.** "Only £70,000 of combined personal savings and no outside investment". Later backed by growth finance from Lloyds Bank (amount not found). REPORTED ([Insider](https://www.insider.co.uk/news/glasgow-graduates-create-growing-vitamin-28794580); [Lloyds case study](https://www.lloydsbank.com/business/resource-centre/case-studies/novomins.html))
- **Problem it claims to solve.** Pills that are hard to swallow (joint, magnesium), the metallic taste of iron, gagging, and the burden of remembering supplements. CONFIRMED (brand "Who We Are" page, via search snippet: [novomins.com/pages/who-we-are](https://novomins.com/pages/who-we-are))
- **One-line positioning (brand's own words).** *"Great tasting, all-natural, fruit-flavoured, nutritional gummies that help to make the healthy choice the obvious choice."* CONFIRMED (same page). The brand also repeatedly calls itself *"the UK's leading gummy vitamin brand"* (self-claim, not independently verified).
- **Values.** Certified B Corp since May 2023 under Innocent Health Limited, with a B Impact score of 88.1. Vegan Society certified on many SKUs, PETA cruelty-free, and no titanium dioxide. REPORTED ([B Corp directory](https://www.bcorporation.net//find-a-b-corp/company/innocent-health-limited); [Dynamic Business AU](https://dynamicbusiness.com.au/featured/the-all-natural-vitamin-brand-disrupting-the-market.html))
- **Tone.** Friendly and sensory ("delight the senses"), backed by "doctors, pharmacologists, neuroscientists and nutritionists" and "in-house regulatory specialists". REPORTED ([Lloyds case study](https://www.lloydsbank.com/business/resource-centre/case-studies/novomins.html))
- **Target customer.** Mainly women aged about 25-50 with life-stage needs (pregnancy/folic acid, PMS, intimate health, menopause, beauty), plus parents (kids range). The Sunday Times 100 coverage describes "a wide range of gummy supplements specifically designed for women". REPORTED ([NutraIngredients, 9 Jun 2026](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/06/09/health-and-wellness-brands-take-center-stage-on-times-100-list/)). Age band is INFERRED from the range.
- **Location note.** The press frames the brand as "Glasgow graduates", but the company is London-based (King's Road). The Scottish/Irish founder identity is used tactically in regional press (Scottish business titles, *Irish Post* "Irish-born" founder). INFERRED

**Products that do NOT fit the story (INFERRED):**
- **NAD+, Shilajit, Maca, Libido, ACV.** These chase TikTok-era trends and weaken the "clinician-led, established nutritional science" framing used with Lloyds and in the press. NAD+ in gummy form has weak evidence for oral bioavailability. Shilajit has known heavy-metal contamination concerns in the category.
- **"Endo" Gummies** (inositol 40:1, B6, turmeric, bacteria). The name points strongly to endometriosis, a disease. UK/EU rules prohibit food supplements from implying they prevent or treat disease (Retained Reg. 1924/2006; ASA CAP Code 15). This is a claims-risk SKU, though no ASA ruling against Novomins was found (search of ASA ruling coverage, 2026-10-06).
- **Men's Multivitamin.** Consistent with the format story but inconsistent with the women-led positioning that the press emphasises.

## 5. Coherence

**Where things reinforce each other:**
- **Format, problem and shelf.** The founding problem (forgotten, unpleasant pills) leads to the gummy-only format. That format suits impulse, fixture-led pharmacy and health-store shelves (Boots, Superdrug, H&B, Chemist Warehouse), where a bright £19.99 tub competes on taste and convenience. INFERRED
- **Clinical founders and pharmacy retail.** "Dr" founders, in-house regulatory staff and B Corp status are the signals pharmacy buyers and Chemist Warehouse value. The Lloyds case study frames formulations as "aligned with established nutritional science rather than trends". REPORTED and INFERRED
- **Women's need-state taxonomy.** Bio-Balance, PMS, Folic, Endo and Collagen map onto how UK pharmacy fixtures are organised ("women's vitamins"). That helps listings and navigation. INFERRED
- **Bootstrapped story and PR.** Repeated milestone PR (sales figures, gummies per second, Times 100) feeds retailer confidence and regional press pick-up. REPORTED

**Where they pull apart:**
- **Science claim vs trend SKUs.** NAD+, shilajit, maca and libido contradict the "not trends alone" positioning. INFERRED
- **Dose vs promise.** Some gummies carry low doses where the evidence base needs grams (collagen at 980mg). Gummies also limit dose generally (sugar or polyol load per serving). This conflicts with "science-backed" messaging. INFERRED
- **Disease-adjacent naming** ("Endo", "PMS") creates claims risk under UK/EU rules. INFERRED
- **"UK's leading gummy vitamin brand" and "15 million customers"** are unverifiable self-claims. Vitabiotics, Bears With Benefits, Vitamin Friends and Haliborange are large UK gummy competitors. INFERRED
- **US ambition vs execution.** A US push was announced around 2022, but by 2026 the only evidence is a third-party-sold Amazon listing. REPORTED and INFERRED
- **Women-first vs everyone.** The kids and men's lines broaden reach but blur the brand's identity. INFERRED

*Back-of-envelope check (INFERRED):* one bottle every 10 seconds is about 3.15m bottles a year. At about £10-12 net to the brand per £19.99 RRP bottle, that implies roughly £30-40m retail-equivalent sales a year in the UK. This is consistent with a Times 100 ranking, but it is an analyst estimate from a company velocity claim, not a reported figure.

**Most plausible success drivers:**

1. **Early, gummy-only, need-state range aimed at women (INFERRED, with REPORTED support).** A wide set of £19.99 women's need-state SKUs gave retailers a ready-made fixture block, and gave consumers a "one brand for my concerns" choice.
   - *Contradicting evidence would be:* sales concentrated in generic SKUs (magnesium, multivitamin) rather than women's lines, or women's SKUs being delisted.
   - *Found:* none, but no SKU-level sales data exists. Magnesium being the lead Superdrug SKU suggests that generic minerals may matter at least as much.
2. **Moving fast from DTC to pharmacy retail, with financing (REPORTED).** DTC and Amazon reviews (Trustpilot 4.8, Amazon 5.0) gave proof of demand. Chemist Warehouse IE (2022), then UK multiples and AU, followed within about three years, funded by Lloyds rather than equity.
   - *Contradicting evidence would be:* retail growth bought through heavy promotion with falling margin or ranging losses.
   - *Found:* deep H&B multibuy promotions (15-20% off) are visible. No margin data. Partly unresolved.
3. **Clinical-founder credibility and B Corp as trust shortcuts (REPORTED).** "Dr" founders, a pharmacology co-founder, in-house regulatory staff and B Corp (May 2023) support pharmacy acceptance.
   - *Contradicting evidence would be:* consumer-facing communications that rarely use the founders, or regulatory enforcement.
   - *Found:* no ASA ruling found. The extent of consumer-facing founder content is unverified, because social was not found.
4. **Taste and format solve pill fatigue, driving repeat purchase (INFERRED).** The founding insight (unfinished tubs) is a compliance insight, and gummies plus subscriptions (up to 25% off) target repeat purchase.
   - *Contradicting evidence would be:* low repeat rates or reviews complaining about sugar, taste or price.
   - *Found:* none. No retention data was found.
5. **Category tailwind (REPORTED).** Gummy supplement growth accelerated to 7.9% in 2025, driven by TikTok ([New Hope](https://www.newhope.com/market-data-and-analysis/analysts-take-tiktok-drives-gummy-supplement-growth-defying-expectations-of-market-slowdown); US data). If the tailwind explains most of the growth, the brand-specific drivers above matter less.
   - *Contradicting evidence would be:* Novomins growing faster than UK gummy category growth, which would point to brand-specific drivers.
   - *Found:* a Times 100 ranking implies growth far above the category rate, so this is a partial contradiction (supportive of brand-specific drivers).
