The Problem This Solves for MRO

MRO sources specialized liquids from overseas OEMs — while running a negative cash-flow cycle and a documented 2021 recall history.

Why This Is a Structural Fit

Three Pain Points, One Fit

Overseas Lead Time & QC Risk
China-based OEM partners extend timelines and quality-control distance.
Cold-Chain & Shelf-Life Exposure
Liquid portfolio flagged internally for temp-control risk, incl. a 2021 recall.
Cash-Flow-Dependent Terms
MRO sells before it pays manufacturers — payment flexibility is a gating factor.

TOSLA Fit

A U.S.-based, quality-mature liquid/liposomal manufacturer that cuts lead time, supports FDA/ISO compliance, and fits negotiated payment terms — a structural fit, not a cheaper-vendor pitch.
