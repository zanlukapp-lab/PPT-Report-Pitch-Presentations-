## Positioning

TOSLA: Turning Science Into Products Consumers Actually Use.

TOSLA is a liquid supplement innovation, development and manufacturing
company. It turns scientifically validated ingredients into commercially
successful consumer products by combining formulation expertise, sensory
engineering, clinical validation, regulatory understanding and scalable
manufacturing in one integrated platform.

Unlike traditional manufacturers that wait for a specification, TOSLA gets
involved early: evaluating scientific opportunities, identifying consumer
needs, assessing market potential, developing formulations, validating
efficacy, optimising sensory performance and preparing products for
commercial scale. Brands move faster, with less development risk and
stronger competitive positioning.

In one sentence: TOSLA transforms validated science into scalable liquid
wellness products by combining formulation expertise, sensory engineering,
clinical validation and manufacturing into one integrated development
platform.

## What TOSLA does

Develops, validates, manufactures and scales liquid nutraceutical and
nutricosmetic products, from concept to global commercial production.

Capabilities:
- Product concept development
- Formulation science
- Liquid supplement development
- Sensory optimisation and flavour engineering
- Stability and compatibility testing
- Clinical and consumer validation
- Regulatory support
- Packaging selection and optimisation
- Manufacturing scale-up
- Commercial production
- Strategic innovation partnerships

These run as one development and commercialisation process, removing
friction between R&D, manufacturing, regulatory and commercial execution.

## Core strength: liquid format expertise

Delivery format drives product success: consumer adoption, consistency of
use and experience matter as much as ingredients. Expertise covers
ingredient compatibility, formulation stability, active delivery and
long-term performance in liquid systems, so complex products stay stable,
effective and enjoyable every day.

## Three pillars

- **Validation** - does the product work? Clinical studies, consumer
  studies, scientific partnerships and evidence generation, used as a
  decision-making framework, not a marketing tool.
- **Palatability** - will consumers take it every day? Heavy investment in
  flavour engineering and sensory optimisation.
- **Simplicity** - can it become part of daily life? Products designed for
  routines, adherence and repeat use.

## What makes TOSLA different

- Optimises for consumer behaviour (adoption and adherence), not only
  formulation performance.
- Combines science and commercial reality: stability over shelf life,
  regulatory compliance, efficient scale-up and user experience addressed
  together, not in sequence.
- Builds around proof, so claims withstand consumer and regulatory scrutiny.
