## Positioning

TOSLA: Turning Science Into Products Consumers Actually Use.

TOSLA is a liquid supplement innovation, development and manufacturing
company. It turns scientifically validated ingredients into commercially
successful consumer products by combining formulation expertise, sensory
engineering, clinical validation, regulatory understanding and scalable
manufacturing in one integrated platform.

Unlike traditional manufacturers that wait for a specification, TOSLA gets
involved early: evaluating scientific opportunities, identifying consumer
needs, assessing market potential, developing formulations, validating
efficacy, optimising sensory performance and preparing products for
commercial scale. Brands move faster, with less development risk and
stronger competitive positioning.

In one sentence: TOSLA transforms validated science into scalable liquid
wellness products by combining formulation expertise, sensory engineering,
clinical validation and manufacturing into one integrated development
platform.

## What TOSLA does

Develops, validates, manufactures and scales liquid nutraceutical and
nutricosmetic products, from concept to global commercial production.

Capabilities:
- Product concept development
- Formulation science
- Liquid supplement development
- Sensory optimisation and flavour engineering
- Stability and compatibility testing
- Clinical and consumer validation
- Regulatory support
- Packaging selection and optimisation
- Manufacturing scale-up
- Commercial production
- Strategic innovation partnerships

These run as one development and commercialisation process, removing
friction between R&D, manufacturing, regulatory and commercial execution.

## Core strength: liquid format expertise

Delivery format drives product success: consumer adoption, consistency of
use and experience matter as much as ingredients. Expertise covers
ingredient compatibility, formulation stability, active delivery and
long-term performance in liquid systems, so complex products stay stable,
effective and enjoyable every day.

## Three pillars

- **Validation** - does the product work? Clinical studies, consumer
  studies, scientific partnerships and evidence generation, used as a
  decision-making framework, not a marketing tool.
- **Palatability** - will consumers take it every day? Heavy investment in
  flavour engineering and sensory optimisation.
- **Simplicity** - can it become part of daily life? Products designed for
  routines, adherence and repeat use.

## What makes TOSLA different

- Optimises for consumer behaviour (adoption and adherence), not only
  formulation performance.
- Combines science and commercial reality: stability over shelf life,
  regulatory compliance, efficient scale-up and user experience addressed
  together, not in sequence.
- Builds around proof, so claims withstand consumer and regulatory scrutiny.

## VELIOUS(TM) technology

Sensory optimisation technology that makes high-potency formulations
enjoyable: manages bitterness, off-notes and other sensory problems of
high-performance actives, keeping strong active profiles with a better
consumer experience.

## Innovation platform

Accumulated ingredient knowledge, formulation expertise, stability data,
packaging knowledge, scientific evidence, clinical experience, sensory
optimisation capabilities and manufacturing know-how. Shortens development
timelines and reduces uncertainty.

## Why brands partner with TOSLA

Reduce development risk; accelerate time to market; improve
differentiation; support claims with evidence; optimise product
experience; scale from concept to commercial production; build products
consumers keep using.

## Vision

The future of wellness belongs to products that combine science, user
experience and commercial scalability. Mission: transform promising
ingredients into products people trust, enjoy and use consistently.

## Public facts (web search, 2026-10-06)

The user pointed to https://toslagroup.com/ as the full source. This
environment's network policy blocks that site, so the facts below come
from search-result extracts of trade press and TOSLA's older site
(toslanutricosmetics.com). Tag: REPORTED unless noted. Check against
toslagroup.com before quoting externally.

- Founded 2014 to develop and produce liquid nutricosmetics; based in
  Ajdovscina, Slovenia; more than 10 years' experience with strong export
  presence. (toslanutricosmetics.com/about-us; NutraIngredients supplier
  page; Cosmetics Business company page)
- Describes itself as a "liquid first CDMO partner": white-label and custom
  co-development of liquid supplements for premium beauty, health,
  wellness and retail own brands. (NutraIngredients supplier page)
- Certified B Corporation (eco-friendly and upcycled ingredient sourcing,
  responsible manufacturing). (GCI Magazine; Cosmetics Business)
- Expanding from nutricosmetics into full-spectrum nutraceuticals, aimed at
  the gap between efficacy and adherence. (Cosmetics & Toiletries / GCI,
  "From Nutricosmetics to Nutraceuticals: Inside TOSLA's Next Chapter of
  Scalable Innovation")
- TOSLA 3 "super factory" next to the new Pavlina headquarters combines
  manufacturing, R&D and strategic development; total capacity 45 million
  bottles a year; four 25-ton production vessels, fully automated filling
  line, turbo-mixing premix vessel. Production utilisation kept below 40%
  so capacity is immediately available for fast-growing partners and new
  launches. (National Law Review press release; Cosmetics & Toiletries)
- VELIOUS(TM) is an in-house masking/flavour technology that makes
  formulations taste good without relying on added sugar, e.g. to balance
  collagen's taste. (Vitafoods Europe 2026 exhibitor listing)
- Clinical evidence: a series of placebo-controlled, peer-reviewed trials
  over about three years on combinations of hydrolysed collagen,
  hyaluronic acid and vitamin C, nearly 300 participants in total. One
  double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised four-way 12-week study (10g
  hydrolysed fish collagen; 5g or 10g collagen with 1.5g MSM) published in
  the Journal of Functional Foods reported improved dermis density, skin
  texture and fewer wrinkles. Study pages: CP10000, CPM6500.
  (Cosmetics Business; Cosmetics & Toiletries; NutraIngredients, 24 Oct
  2023; toslanutricosmetics.com)
- 21 Apr 2026: launched with Geltor the first vegan "signaling collagen"
  liquid supplement - a 30 mL daily shot with 1g PrimaColl (type 21
  biotech collagen) and VELIOUS(TM), cherry-raspberry. Shown at Vitafoods
  Europe (Barcelona, 5-7 May 2026) and NYSCC Suppliers' Day (NYC, 19-20
  May 2026). (PR Newswire; Geltor; Nutrition Insight)
- Partner brands: formulations "loved by millions of returning customers
  across partners' brands"; no client brand names found in public sources.
  Related white-label site: whitelabelcollagen.com.
